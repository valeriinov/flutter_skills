# Voice

- Talk to the user directly, as "you"; the app speaks as "we" only in success messages.
- Sentence case everywhere: buttons, titles, tabs. Terms are lowercase in the middle of a sentence.
- Buttons are a verb, or a verb and an object: "Book", "Cancel booking".
- An error says what happened and what to do next. It never blames the user.
- No exclamation marks, except in a success message.
- Money and anything that cannot be undone are written in full: the amount, the moment, the consequence.
